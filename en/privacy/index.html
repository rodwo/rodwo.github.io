Lifnook Studio App Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Scope

This policy is provided by Lifnook Studio and covers Food Diary (com.acefon.fooddiary), Paw & Bowl (com.acefon.pawandbowl), Rent Manager (com.acefon.rentmanager), Daily Ledger (com.lifnook.dailyledger), and this portfolio website. The services and processing described below apply according to your app, version, platform, and enabled features.

Records you enter and their purposes

Everyday records are stored on your device to provide record lookup, calculations, charts, reminders, or management features. The four apps currently require no app account and offer no self-operated cloud synchronization for these records.

Food Diary stores food, calories, nutrients, portions, notes, water, and weight records, together with height, age, sex, activity level, and preferences you enter. These support your diary, trends, and calculations.

Paw & Bowl stores pet names and species, feeding plans, meal and weight records, notes, and preferences for care journals, charts, and feeding reminders.

Rent Manager stores room numbers, tenant names or preferred names, property notes, rent amounts, billing intervals, rental periods, due dates, collected amounts, payment dates, and notes for property management, bills, payment status, statistics, and reports. Its Web version uses local browser storage. Obtain appropriate authorization and provide necessary notice when entering other people’s information.

Daily Ledger stores expense amounts, categories, dates, optional notes, and language and appearance settings on your device for record lookup, monthly and category summaries, and backup and restore. Expense records and backups are not sent to advertising or purchase-verification services.

The apps do not send Paw & Bowl care records or Rent Manager tenant, property, bill, or payment-note content to our servers, advertising services, or purchase-verification services. Food Diary’s online food lookup is described below.

Permissions and online features

Food Diary uses the camera to scan food barcodes without saving or uploading the scanner feed. You can also enter a barcode manually. Online lookups send barcodes, product search terms, query language, and necessary parameters to Open Food Facts over HTTPS. That service also receives connection information such as your IP address. Offline food searches run on your device.

Paw & Bowl uses the system photo picker to access the pet avatar you select and stores a copy in its local folder. You can remove or replace it. Android schedules feeding notifications on the device. Manage notification permission in system settings.

Backup, restore, or export features read files you select or pass files to a storage location or sharing service you choose. Manage app permissions in device settings.

Permanent ad removal and advertising services

The Android apps offer permanent ad removal through a one-time purchase, with no annual fees or recurring subscription charges.

If you use an ad-supported version without ad removal enabled or an older version that displayed ads, Google AdMob may process IP addresses, advertising or device identifiers, interactions, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. Daily Ledger loads ads according to applicable consent settings and offers ad privacy options when required.

One-time purchases and restoration

Google Play handles Android payments. Versions with purchases enabled use RevenueCat to fetch products, verify transactions, check entitlements, and restore purchases. These services may process randomly generated app user identifiers, product codes, purchase receipts or tokens, transaction status, and device, app, or network information needed to provide the service. The apps do not receive full payment card numbers or payment passwords.

See Google Play or the in-app purchase screen for pricing and purchase entitlements.

Restoring purchases restores verified feature access. Manage everyday records separately using the relevant backup or restore features.

Backups, retention, and deletion

Supported versions can export or restore JSON backups. Some paid features allow CSV export. Backups and reports may contain personal, health, pet, tenant, or financial information, so store them securely. Daily Ledger JSON backups contain expense records and language and appearance settings. Restoring a backup replaces current expense records. Paw & Bowl JSON backups exclude avatar photos and membership entitlements. Rent Manager CSV reports cannot restore data, and restoring JSON overwrites current rent records on that device. Food Diary and Rent Manager export formats have no additional app-implemented encryption.

Local records remain until you delete them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Clear website storage to remove Web-version records. Archiving a property in Rent Manager retains its history, and deleting a payment does not delete the bill for rent due. Manage or delete system backups, exported files, shared copies, and cloud-storage copies separately.

Google, RevenueCat, and other services may retain transaction, advertising, security, or support information under their purposes, policies, and applicable law. Local deletion does not automatically remove their records or copies already shared.

Security, providers, and international processing

Purchase verification, Food Diary’s online lookup, and advertising features in ad-supported versions use network services. Google, RevenueCat, Open Food Facts, website hosting, and storage or sharing services you choose process information needed for their respective roles and may process it outside your country or region. Processing locations, retention, and safeguards depend on the service and applicable requirements.

Use a device lock and store backups in access-protected locations. Security also depends on your device, operating system, and file-management practices. Storage and transmission methods carry risks.

Your choices and data requests

Use available app features to view, edit, export, or delete records, and manage permissions in system settings. Under applicable law, you may contact us to request access, copies, correction, deletion, or restrictions on processing of personal information we actually hold. Reasonable identity or transaction verification may be needed, and necessary records may be retained as required by law.

You manage local records on your device. We cannot remotely read, delete, or restore them. In your initial request, identify the app and platform and avoid including complete health records, tenant lists, backups, or payment information.

Portfolio website information

These portfolio pages provide app and policy information without contact forms, advertising, or visitor analytics scripts. OpenAI Sites hosting and access services may process IP addresses, browser information, request times, and security logs, and may use cookies needed for sign-in, access verification, or security.

The portfolio homepages use Google Fonts and load app icons and screenshots from Google Play’s image service. These requests send Google your IP address and necessary connection information. Store links on the homepages open Google Play. Language switching uses separate URLs and does not write language preferences to cookies or local storage.

Contact, inquiries, and updates

The developer and operator is acefon. For privacy questions, data requests, or concerns about children’s personal information, contact acefon01@gmail.com. We use the email address and content you send to respond, resolve issues, and follow up as necessary, and retain them according to those purposes and applicable obligations.

When features or information practices change, we update this page and its date, providing required notices or obtaining consent where applicable.
