Last updated November 18, 2026

Scope of this policy

This page describes how the acefon portfolio website handles information and provides links to each app’s official privacy policy. This website is maintained by acefon.

Browsing and service providers

The portfolio pages have no contact forms, ads, or visitor analytics scripts. Hosting and access services may process IP addresses, browser information, and request logs to deliver pages, verify access, and maintain security. Their policies govern processing and retention.

Fonts, images, and external links

This website uses Google Fonts and loads app icons and screenshots from Google Play’s image service. When these resources load, your browser sends Google your IP address and necessary connection information. When you follow a Google Play or app policy link, the destination handles information under its own policy.

Languages and browser storage

Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, and English each have separate URLs. Language switching uses links and does not write a language preference to cookies or local storage. The hosting platform may use necessary cookies for sign-in, access control, and security.

Contact and data requests

For privacy questions or data requests, email the contact address below. We use the email address and message you provide to respond to and handle your inquiry. Include only the information needed to address your request.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when website features or information practices change. The current version and its update date will appear on this page.
